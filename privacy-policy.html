Scope and purpose This policy applies to the browser extension YouTube Spatial Audio. The extension processes audio played on YouTube and YouTube Music locally on your device, allowing you to adjust headphone-oriented depth, stereo width, short room ambience and bass punch. 2. Information handled locally To provide its features, the extension uses the following information locally: Audio currently played by supported players, processed in real time through the browser's Web Audio API. Current supported tab URLs and tab identifiers, used to identify supported sites and apply processing and settings to the correct tabs. URLs may include page paths and query parameters. Player and volume-control page elements, media source references and playback state, used to connect audio processing, position the settings button and work with the original volume controls. The page or browser display language, used to localize the interface. Settings interactions, page interactions needed to activate audio processing, and pointer/focus interactions around volume controls, used to perform the requested features. The extension does not accumulate interaction logs. Output device names, device IDs, group IDs and connection state are used to identify the default output and switch device-specific audio settings and custom presets. The extension does not save audio as recordings, perform speech recognition or create transcripts. The optional Identify devices action requests microphone permission on the supported site to reveal output device names and immediately stops the stream. Microphone audio is not saved as recordings, analyzed or transmitted. It does not retrieve a list of past browsing history or persist page URLs or playback history. It has no feature that reads names, email addresses, passwords, authentication cookies, payment details, location, emails or chats. 3. Stored information and storage location The extension stores audio settings including depth, width, room ambience, punch and the spatial-audio on/off preference; one common custom preset and one custom preset per output profile; the automatic connection preference; the slider wheel direction preference; and device profiles including display names, matching output names, audio settings, selection mode and profile management identifiers. Device IDs are only used during detection and are not persisted. Settings may also contain a fixed output-volume value for compatibility. These settings are saved in chrome.storage.local in your browser profile. The extension does not use chrome.storage.sync or send settings to developer servers or cloud synchronization services. Display language, connection state, processed audio and page information are not persistently stored. 4. External transmission and third parties The extension does not transmit, share or sell the information it handles to the developer or third parties. It does not provide advertising, run analytics, collect usage statistics or create tracking identifiers. Translation files and executable code are bundled with the extension and are not retrieved from external servers. Information handling by normal YouTube/YouTube Music playback, Chrome Web Store distribution and updates, and the browser or operating system itself is governed by the respective providers' policies. This policy describes information handling by the extension itself. 5. Permissions The storage permission is used to save and restore settings. Site access is limited to https://www.youtube.com/*, https://youtube.com/* and https://music.youtube.com/* for player audio processing and the settings interface. The extension does not request history, cookies or tabCapture permissions. Microphone permission is requested on the supported site only when you choose Identify devices; normal audio processing and manual profile selection do not require it. 6. Retention, deletion and stopping Saved settings remain in your browser until you change, overwrite or delete them or uninstall the extension. Use Delete selected profile in device settings to remove a device profile and its custom preset. Uninstalling removes the settings stored in chrome.storage.local. Use the purple Stop button to stop audio processing in the current tab. Turn off automatic connection to disable it on newly opened pages. Stopping microphone access does not itself revoke site permission stored in the browser. You can revoke microphone permission in the browser settings for the relevant site. You can also disable or remove the extension through your browser's extension management page. 7. Limited use The extension uses information only to provide its audio processing and settings features. It adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. User data is not used for advertising, credit assessment, lending or data sales. 8. Changes and contact If features or information handling change, this policy and its last-updated date will be updated. For privacy inquiries, use the contact listed at the beginning of this policy.
